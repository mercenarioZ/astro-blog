Long time no see, lmao. I'm here to update my current status. 

I've changed from the LMK81 to this, the **Lofree Flow Lite**, as my daily keyboard, I use it to handle all the stuff at my office. I've been using this keyboard for 1 month, and it quickly became one of those tools I don't have to think about anymore, which is axactly what I want. It's low-profile, minimal, and feels intentional in both design and typing experience.

The first thing that stands out is how thin and clean it looks on the desk. I can place it perfectly to the front of my MacBook 14-inch, that's the main reason why I chose this keyboard.

I chose the lite version because of its weight, just around half a kilogram, therefore I can carry it to everywhere without worrying about the weight.
